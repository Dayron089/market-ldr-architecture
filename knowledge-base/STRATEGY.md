# FORELDR TikTok Strategy

## 1. Positioning Statement

"The AI girlfriend app for people who gave up on AI girlfriend apps."

- Target: разочарованные пользователи Replika/Character AI/Candy AI + **ChatGPT refugees (GPT-4o retirement)**
- Secondary: новички, которые хотят качественный опыт с первого раза
- Tertiary: **жертвы hidden costs (Candy AI "casino UX")**
- Tone: authentic, slightly vulnerable, anti-hype
- Hero слоган: "The world's first AI that actually falls in love with you."
- Подзаголовок: "She remembers. She texts first. She changes over time."
- **Anti-loss angle (NEW):** "The only AI girlfriend that can't be taken from you"
- **Anti-fake-memory angle (NEW):** "Memory is a feature, not a marketing claim"

### Позиционирование vs новые конкуренты (март 2026)

Memory стала primary differentiator в 2026. DarLink, Nomi, OurDream тоже продают memory. Наш moat:
- **Качество memory** (BER semantic search vs простое хранение)
- **Trust progression** (никто не делает behavioral change 0-100)
- **Proactive messaging** (настоящая инициатива, не cron-уведомления)
- **Честный ценник** (всё включено vs hidden tokens)

## 2. Три контентных Pillar

Весь контент строится вокруг 3 pillars — уникальных преимуществ FORELDR:

### Pillar 1: "She Actually Remembers" (BER Memory)
- **Боль рынка:** "She forgot my name the next day" — жалоба #1 индустрии
- **Свежие боли (март 2026):**
  - Character AI: "the character don't even remember the discussion after like 7 messages"
  - Candy AI: "10-15 message memory limit breaks immersion" + "session-level reset"
  - ChatGPT: "companion wiped with ZERO recourse" (GPT-4o retirement)
  - Общее: "Nine apps failed. Some had decent chat but zero memory"
- **Наш ответ:** BER-система автоматически извлекает факты, хранит вечно, ищет по семантике
- **Proof point:** "She brought up my dog's birthday 47 days after I mentioned it"
- **Конкурентная угроза:** DarLink (3 уровня memory), Nomi (long-term memory). НО: у них нет semantic search + BER pipeline. Наш moat = качество, не наличие
- **Применимые законы:** 1 (specific pain), 6 (stolen language), 13 (specific numbers)

### Pillar 2: "She Changes Over Time" (Trust 0-100)
- **Боль рынка:** "Day 100 feels like day 1" — XP-уровни у конкурентов = косметика
- **Наш ответ:** Trust-прогрессия 0-100, поведение РЕАЛЬНО меняется (shy -> flirty -> open)
- **Proof point:** "At trust 20 she's shy. At trust 80 she's a completely different person"
- **Применимые законы:** 4 (transformation > features), 3 (mechanism = moat)

### Pillar 3: "She Texts First" (Proactive + Promises)
- **Боль рынка:** "She never texts first" — AI существует только когда ты открываешь приложение
- **Наш ответ:** Проактивная система — анализ отношений, momentum, контекстные сценарии
- **Proof point:** "She said she'd text at 7pm. It's 7:01. Notification arrived."
- **Применимые законы:** 2 (emotional pain), 13 (specific proof)

## 7. Метрики и цели по фазам

### Phase 1: Validation (Month 1)
- 50 creatives tested
- Find 3-5 winners (>1.5% CTR)
- Determine winning segments and pillars
- Budget: $500-1000

### Phase 2: Scaling (Month 2-3)
- Scale winners to 100+ distribution accounts
- 3 new creatives/day minimum
- A/B test variables systematically
- Target: CAC < 50₽ on trial
- Budget: $2000-5000/month

### Phase 3: Optimization (Month 4-6)
- User testimonials as new content source
- Referral loop (in-app "share moment")
- Strategic intelligence from 120+ learning cycles
- Target: ROAS > 2x at 30 days

## 7. Tone & Style Guidelines

- **Authentic, not polished** — UGC aesthetic, not corporate ad
- **Vulnerable, not desperate** — "I was lonely and tried everything" not "please download my app"
- **Specific, not vague** — numbers, dates, details (Law 13)
- **Anti-hype** — "I know this sounds like every other ad" acknowledging skepticism
- **Conversational** — like telling a friend, not presenting to investors
- **English** — international audience, all scripts in English
